Lesson 3 Summary

  • Triangle Similarity is the concept of two triangles having the same proportions no matter what size.
  • There are three Theorems that can help prove the similarities between triangles.
  • The Pythagorean Theorem deals with the relationships between the sides of a right triangle.
  • The formula for the theorem equates to c² = a² + b².
