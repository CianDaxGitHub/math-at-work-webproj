Quadratic Equations are polynomial equations with a second degree, which means at least one coefficient or term has the highest power of two.

Standard Form:


In the standard form, x is referred to as a variable, while a, b, and c refer to constants or real numbers, in which a does not equal 0.

Solving quadratic equations involve finding the zeros of the equation; which are values of x that satisfy the equation, in which replacing x with these values should equal to 0. There are at least two roots or zeros in a quadratic equation.
