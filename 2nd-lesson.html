Lesson 2 Summary

  • Exponents are a simplified version of a number's Repeated Multiplication by itself, depicted with a base value, and an exponent to determine the number of times to repeat.
  • There are a series of Rules regarding Exponents that can help simplify expressions with Exponents.
  • Rational Exponents are a form of Fractional Exponents where the numerator is the power, and the denominator is the root.
  • When solving equations involving Rational Exponents, you can use the reciprocal power to cancel out the power of the variable.
