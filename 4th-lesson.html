Lesson 4 Summary

  • Law of Sines are based on right-triangle relationships to allow for solving sides and angles without the need for a right triangle.
  • The Law of Sines are used when there is at least two angles and one side.
  • The Law of Cosines are a feasible alternative if the Sine Law cannot be applied due to a lack of a Side-Angle pair.
  • This Law can be used even if all angles are missing.
